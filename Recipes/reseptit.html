Focaccia

Ingredients

For dough

  • 50 g yeast
  • 7 dl wheat flour
  • 3/4 dl olive oil
  • 4 dl water
  • 1 tsp honey
To the top
  • 3/4 dl olive oil
  • Olives
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Pinch of sea salt
Remember to experiment with the toppings! Focaccia is a versatile bread, so you can pretty much use any toppings you like.

Preparation

  1. Heat the water until it's lukewarm, then put yeast in it.
  2. Add salt. Add flour a bit at a time, remember to mix before adding more. Add honey.
  3. Knead the dough and add the olive oil at the end of kneading.
  4. Take the dough out of the bowl and knead it on an oiled surface for aprox 5 minutes. Let it proof until it's double the size.
  5. Oil the baking sheet/baking dish. Put the dough in so it fills the whole sheet/dish. Add 1/4 dl olive oil. Let it proof under the parchment paper for aprox 40 minutes.
  6. Press dents on the proofed dough and add rest of the olive oil (2/4 dl). Put olives, cherry tomatoes and sea salt on top, press them so they stay better.
  7. Bake at 225°C on the lower middle rack of the oven for 15-20 minutes.
  8. Remove the baked bread from the pan and let it cool before cutting it to serving sizes.
  9. Done!

Naan

Ingredients

For dough
  • 11 g yeast
  • aprox 10 dl wheat flour
  • 200 g turkish yogurt
  • 2 dl water
  • 50 g melted butter
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
To the top
  • Butter

Preparation

  1. Heat the water and yogurt until they're lukewarm. Mix together yeast, salt and sugar.
  2. Add flour a bit at a time, remember to mix before adding more.
  3. Add the melted butter at the end before kneading. Knead the dough until it comes off the sides of the bowl.
  4. Cover the dough with a cloth and let it rise in a warm place for about 30 minutes.
  5. Divide the dough into portions and shape them into rounds, using a rolling pin and flour.
  6. Put the naan on the baking sheet without parchment paper.
  7. Bake the naan at 250°C for about 6 minutes on the top rack of the oven until brown spots appear on the surface.
  8. Remove the naan from the baking sheet and brush it with melted butter. Cover it with parchment paper and cloth to keep them soft.
  9. Done!

Brioche

Ingredients

For dough
  • 25 g yeast
  • 7 dl wheat flour
  • 1 dl milk
  • 150 g melted butter
  • 2 egg
  • 1/2 dl sugar
  • 3/4 tsp salt
To the top
  • 50 g butter
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tbsp vanilla sugar
Greasing
  • 1 yolk
Glazing
  • Powdered sugar

Preparation

  1. Heat the milk until it's lukewarm, then put yeast in it.
  2. Add sugar, salt and eggs. Add half the flour a bit at a time, remember to mix before adding more. Add melted butter and rest of the flour.
  3. Let it rise for aprox 5 minutes. Roll the dough into a rectangle sheets. Fold the sheet into thirds and roll it back to its original size. Repeat this process 2-3 times. Let it rise for 5-10 minutes with a cloth covering it.
  4. Roll out the dough on a floured surface into a rectangle. Spread softened butter over the dough. Sprinkle with sugar evenly. Roll the dough from the long side.
  5. Cut the rolled dough into slices and place them in a greased tin with cut sides up. Let proof for about an hour before greasing with yolk.
  6. Bake in the lower or middle rack of the oven at 220°C for 5 minutes. Reduce the temperature to 200°C and bake aprox 10 minutes untile the buns are golden brown.
  7. Remove the buns from the tin and let them cool. Glaze with powdered sugar.
  8. Done!

Ciabatta

Ingredients

  • 25 g yeast
  • 5 dl cold water
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • aprox 9 dl wheat flour
  • 1 tbsp syrup
  • 1 dl rye flour

Preparation

  1. Put yeast in the cold water. Add salt and approximately half of the flour.
  2. Mix the dough into pasty mixture. Add the rye flour, syrup, oil and gradually add the rest of the flour.
  3. The dough should be as soft as possible. Sprinkle flour over the surface and cover the bowl with a cloth.
  4. Let it proof for 1-2 hours.
  5. Put the dough onto a floured surface and knead out the air bubbles. Divide the dough into two pieces.
  6. Shape the dough into two long loaves and place them on a baking sheet and flatten them.
  7. Cover them with a cloth and let it proof for 30 minutes.
  8. Bake the bread for about 20 minutes at 250 degrees on the lowest rack in the oven.
  9. Place a bowl of water in the oven while the bread is baking to create crispy crust.
  10. The bread is done when it sounds hollow when tapped on the bottom.
